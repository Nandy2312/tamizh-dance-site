About me

Hi, I'm Tamizh.

I've been locking since 2013. I started in Trichy with the Nbeeterz crew and today I dance in Chennai with Lockin Gundargal. Locking is a 1970s funk style built on freezes, sharp points and big personality, and I've learned it from international artists including my master Jimmy Scoo B Doo.

  • Solo locking champion · Indian Premiere Locking, 8th edition
  • Locking winner · Battle Yaa Skills 2023, Bangalore
  • Winner · Kalam 2023 (judged by Oskar Malinga, Sweden)
  • Judge at 9+ battles, including BOC Vol-5, Chennai
  • Workshop artist · Battle Yaa Skills Camp 2025, Trichy, Bangalore, Kerala

How I teach

Foundations first, then freedom. Every class starts with groove and counts, builds one move at a time, and ends with a short cypher so you get used to dancing in front of people from day one.

FAQ

Questions, answered

I've never danced. Can I join?

Yes. The beginner course starts from the basic bounce and counts.

What is locking?

A 1970s funk dance style built on sudden freezes ("locks"), sharp points and big personality. It's energetic, fun and great for beginners.

Which age groups do you teach?

Adults on the main course, with kids and teens batches and private classes for any age. Message us for the next batch.

How long is the course?

Three months of weekend classes, ending with a stage showcase.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes, loose pants, and clean indoor shoes with some grip.

Can I try a class before paying?

Yes. Book a free trial class on WhatsApp and decide after.

Do you teach online or outside Chennai?

Private classes can run online, and Tamizh has taught workshops in Trichy, Bangalore and Kerala. Ask about your city.

What if I miss a class?

Let us know on WhatsApp and we'll help you catch up in the other weekend batch where possible.
